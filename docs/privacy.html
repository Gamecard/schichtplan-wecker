Privacy Policy

“Schichtwecker” app (Android) · Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: The app does not collect any personal data, does not send anything to the internet and contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app has no internet permission and therefore cannot technically send any data at all.

1. Controller

Responsible for this app within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
Codewes · Email: omarhirthe@gmail.com

2. Data the app processes

The app only stores what you enter yourself, and only locally on your device:

Shift scheduleStart week, rotation, swapped shifts, exceptions such as vacation or sick leave, German state (for public holidays)
AlarmsWake-up times, scheduled alarms, pre-alarm, snooze time and other alarm settings
RingtoneIf you choose your own ringtone: a reference to that file (the file itself is not copied)
Appearance and languageLight/dark mode, design and app language; if you load your own translation file, a copy of that file

Entries such as “sick” are part of your private shift schedule. They never leave the device and are neither viewed nor analysed by the developer.

3. No transfer, no tracking

  • No data is transferred to the developer or to third parties.
  • No advertising, no analytics, crash reporting or tracking services.
  • No user account, no sign-in.

4. Permissions and what they are for

Exact alarmsTrigger alarms exactly at the set time
Notifications, full-screen notificationShow the ringing alarm, even on the lock screen
Foreground service (media playback)Play the alarm sound until you turn it off or snooze
Vibration, keep awakeVibrate and keep the device awake while the alarm rings
Run at startupReschedule alarms after the device restarts

5. Backup and export by you

With “Save profile” or “Export calendar” you can save your data as a file. Where the file is stored and whether it is shared is decided solely by you through the Android file dialog. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app data in your own device backup (e.g. your Google account); this backup is operated by Google or your device manufacturer, not by the developer.

6. Deletion

In the app under “Schedule → App → Reset everything” you delete all entries and alarms. When you uninstall the app, Android removes all app data from the device.

7. Your rights

Since the developer does not receive or store any data about you, there is nothing on the developer's side to disclose, correct or delete. Regardless of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Google Play

When you download the app via Google Play, Google processes data according to its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. The developer has no influence on this.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that requires this policy to be updated, this page will be updated.
